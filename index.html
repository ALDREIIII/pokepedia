    About Us

    Mission

    Give old fans and newcomers clear, accurate information about every Pokémon in Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald.

    Vision

    Gen 3 Poképedia aims to build a targeted, visually appealing digital encyclopedia dedicated strictly to the original Hoenn experience across Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald.

    Backstory

    Created to fill the gap left by modern platforms that mix generation data, this site was built from the ground up using modern HTML5 and CSS Grid to offer a clean, dedicated reference tool for classic Hoenn fans.

    Future

    The platform will continue to expand its Hoenn coverage with interactive region maps, full move sets, and comprehensive character guides to serve as the ultimate retro reference tool.

    FAQ

    Why are FireRed and LeafGreen missing?

    This site focuses only on the original Hoenn games.

    Where does the Pokémon data come from?

    Stats, types, descriptions, and sprites load from the free PokéAPI.

    What is the National Dex option?

    It lists all 386 Generation 3 Pokémon instead of only the 202 in Hoenn.
